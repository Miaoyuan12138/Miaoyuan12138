# Hi, I'm Miaoyuan Wen 👋

I'm an **S.M. student in Health Data Science at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health**, interested in building trustworthy data and machine-learning systems for **health, biology, and intelligent systems**.

My work spans biomedical data integration, population-health modeling, scientific data systems, wearable data, machine-learning validation, and embodied AI.

### Research interests

* Health data science & epidemiology
* Biomedical and biological data systems
* Machine learning reliability & validation
* Scientific data quality, provenance & reproducibility
* Digital health & wearable data
* Responsible and interpretable AI
* Embodied AI & robotics

### Selected work

**[Health Risk Modeling](https://github.com/Miaoyuan12138/health-risk-modeling)**
Geographic validation, data provenance, and uncertainty in county-level diabetes prediction.

**[Personal Movement Atlas](https://github.com/Miaoyuan12138/Personal-Movement-Atlas)**
A privacy-aware pipeline for Apple Watch, WHOOP, GPX, and personal movement data.

**[MOP-ECDF](https://github.com/Miaoyuan12138/MOP-ECDF)**
Experimental work on dictionary encoding, compact data representation, and ECDF approximation.
